Privacy Policy

ResumeForge AI processes resumes and job descriptions to provide ATS optimization. We store your account, resumes, job descriptions, and optimization results to deliver the service.

You can delete individual resumes and optimizations, export your data via /api/auth/account/export, or delete your account via /api/auth/account (DELETE). Account deletion removes associated data.

We do not sell personal data. Resume contents are sent to our AI provider (Groq) solely to generate results and are not retained by us beyond your account. Uploaded files are validated and size-limited.

Retention: data is kept while your account exists; deleted on account deletion. Backups may retain data for a limited period.

Last updated: 2026.
